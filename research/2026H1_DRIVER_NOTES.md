# Samyang Foods — 2026 H1 Revenue Driver Notes

_Last updated: 2026-09-08_

## 1. What the first pass says

The first-pass evidence suggests that the forecast should be built around **overseas demand + production capacity + operating efficiency**, not a simple historical revenue CAGR.

### A. Overseas sales are the dominant growth engine

- 2025 consolidated revenue: KRW 2.352tn.
- Reported 2025 overseas sales: about KRW 1.884tn, roughly 80% of revenue.
- 2026 Q1 revenue: KRW 714.4bn; overseas sales: KRW 585.0bn.
- 2026 Q2 revenue: KRW 770.3bn; overseas sales: KRW 645.8bn.
- 2026 H1 overseas sales therefore equal KRW 1.231tn, about 82.9% of H1 revenue.

This means the first forecast split should be **Domestic / Overseas**, then the overseas leg should be decomposed by region.

### B. Regional mix matters

2026 Q1 reported regional sales:
- United States: KRW 185.3bn (+37% YoY)
- China: KRW 171.3bn (+36% YoY)
- Europe: KRW 77.3bn (+215% YoY)

For 2026 Q2, post-results research reported YoY growth of:
- United States: +54.3%
- China: +44.1%
- Europe: +61.0%

Europe is growing from a smaller base but is the clearest incremental growth region. The US and China remain the larger regional anchors.

### C. Capacity is a real operating driver

- Miryang Plant 2 began operating in 2025 and has annual capacity of up to 830m ramen units.
- Miryang Plants 1 and 2 together can produce up to 1.58bn units annually.
- Samyang Foods' 2026 value-up disclosure sets a target of increasing production volume by 40.2% versus the existing base.
- The Jiaxing plant in China is planned for completion in January 2027. The company's July 2025 official release stated six lines, KRW 201.4bn investment, and up to 840m Buldak units per year.

For valuation, 2026 should reflect the **ramp of Miryang Plant 2**, while 2027 onward needs a separate China-capacity step-up.

### D. Margin should not be extrapolated mechanically

- 2025 operating margin: about 22.3%.
- 2026 Q1 operating margin: about 24.8%.
- 2026 Q2 operating margin: about 22.9%.
- 2026 H1 operating margin: about 23.8%.

The company attributed Q2 profitability to overseas-channel improvement, production efficiency, and FX, while higher labor/marketing spending remained a cost pressure. A portfolio-quality model should therefore forecast margin from **mix / production efficiency / marketing / raw-material / FX assumptions**, not simply hold Q1 margin flat.

## 2. First forecast architecture

### Revenue
1. Domestic revenue
2. Overseas revenue
   - United States
   - China
   - Europe
   - Other overseas

### Operating margin
- regional/product mix
- utilization / production efficiency
- FX
- raw-material and logistics cost
- marketing / SG&A

### Capacity bridge
- 2025: Miryang Plant 2 starts contributing
- 2026: utilization ramp
- 2027: Jiaxing China plant planned to come online

## 3. What is still missing before the actual forecast

Do **not** set final 2026E-2030E growth rates yet. We still need:
- 2025 and 2026H1 product-category sales
- detailed plant utilization / production figures from filings
- CAPEX, depreciation, working-capital data
- 2025 regional sales base where disclosed
- ASP / price-mix evidence
- raw-material and logistics-cost trend
- FX sensitivity

Those items will feed the DCF and RIM assumptions.
