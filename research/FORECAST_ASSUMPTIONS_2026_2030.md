# Samyang Foods 2026E-2030E Forecast Assumptions

**Status:** first-pass independent model. These are project assumptions, not company guidance and not copied analyst consensus.

## 1. Current operating anchor

2026H1 consolidated results:
- Revenue: KRW 1,484.7bn
- Operating profit: KRW 353.3bn
- Operating margin: 23.8%
- Net income: KRW 282.1bn

2026H1 simplified core working capital:
- Accounts receivable: KRW 256.4bn
- Inventory: KRW 277.6bn
- Accounts payable: KRW 174.2bn
- Core NWC = AR + inventory - AP = ~KRW 359.8bn
- 2025 year-end core NWC was ~KRW 300.6bn

2026H1 investing/capacity evidence:
- PPE acquisition: KRW 103.3bn, versus KRW 324.9bn in 2025H1
- PPE depreciation: KRW 25.3bn in 2026H1
- 2025 was an unusually heavy expansion-CAPEX year, so the 2025 CAPEX/sales ratio is not used as steady state.

## 2. Capacity logic

- Miryang Plant 2 was completed in June 2025 and is still a key source of incremental export capacity.
- Samyang Foods' first overseas manufacturing base in Jiaxing, China is scheduled for completion in January 2027.
- Company guidance for the Jiaxing project: KRW 201.4bn total investment, six lines, up to 840 million Buldak units annually, all targeted at the China domestic market.
- This creates a clear structural break in 2027: China can increasingly be supplied locally while Korean capacity can support the Americas and Europe.

## 3. Base case

Revenue:
- 2026E: KRW 3.10tn
- 2027E: +20%
- 2028E: +16%
- 2029E: +12%
- 2030E: +9%

The 2026 estimate implies H2 revenue of ~KRW 1.62tn, about 27% YoY versus 2025H2. This is slower than 2026H1 growth and therefore assumes normalization rather than perpetual hyper-growth.

Operating margin:
- 23.5%, 23.8%, 24.0%, 23.8%, 23.5%

Margin is anchored around the 2026H1 result. The model does not assume indefinite margin expansion.

CAPEX:
- 2026E: maintenance 100 + growth 150 = KRW 250bn
- 2027E: 120 + 60 = KRW 180bn
- 2028E: 140 + 30 = KRW 170bn
- 2029E: 155 + 25 = KRW 180bn
- 2030E: 170 + 20 = KRW 190bn

This explicitly separates maintenance and growth CAPEX. Growth CAPEX falls after the Miryang/Jiaxing expansion phase while maintenance rises with the asset base.

Core NWC / sales:
- 12.1%, 11.8%, 11.5%, 11.2%, 11.0%

This assumes gradual working-capital efficiency as overseas distribution and inventory operations mature.

## 4. Bull case

- Stronger U.S./Europe sell-through and successful China localization.
- 2026E revenue KRW 3.25tn; 2027-2030 growth 26%, 20%, 15%, 11%.
- Operating margin peaks around 25.3%.
- Higher growth CAPEX is retained to support additional capacity.

## 5. Bear case

- Export growth decelerates faster, margin normalizes and operating leverage is weaker.
- 2026E revenue KRW 2.95tn; 2027-2030 growth 12%, 10%, 8%, 6%.
- Operating margin falls from 22.0% to 20.0% by 2030.
- Growth CAPEX is reduced because capacity expansion needs are lower.

## 6. Items that still need refinement

1. Revenue forecast by U.S. / China / Europe / Other instead of only consolidated growth.
2. Raw-material, FX, freight and marketing assumptions for EBIT margin.
3. Full D&A reconciliation including right-of-use and intangible amortization.
4. Full operating working capital beyond the simplified AR + inventory - AP proxy.
5. WACC, terminal growth and net debt for DCF.
6. Forward peer PER set.
7. Equity / ROE roll-forward for RIM.

The model should be updated whenever a new filing invalidates an assumption.
