# Project Plan

## 1. Question
Can Samyang Foods' future earnings be explained with observable business drivers, and what equity value follows from those drivers?

## 2. Evidence hierarchy
1. Regulatory filings (DART)
2. Company IR materials
3. KRX disclosures
4. Official macro / trade statistics
5. Secondary research only where primary data is unavailable

## 3. Historical dataset
Target history: 2021-2026H1

### Income statement
- Revenue
- COGS
- Gross profit
- SG&A
- Operating profit
- Pretax income
- Net income

### Balance sheet
- Cash
- Inventory
- Receivables
- Payables
- Debt
- Equity

### Cash flow
- CFO
- D&A
- CAPEX
- FCF

### Business drivers
- Domestic / overseas sales
- Regional sales
- product / brand mix
- production capacity
- utilization
- pricing / ASP proxies
- FX
- input-cost proxies

## 4. Forecast design
Forecast horizon: 3-5 years.

### Base case
Most plausible assumptions supported by filings and operational data.

### Bull case
Faster overseas growth, stronger utilization / mix, better margins.

### Bear case
Slower overseas growth, margin normalization, weaker utilization, FX/input-cost pressure.

## 5. Valuation
- PER: peer-based relative valuation
- RIM: book value + residual income
- DCF: FCFF-based intrinsic valuation
- sensitivity tables required

## 6. Deliverables
- reproducible notebooks
- cleaned CSVs
- documented sources
- valuation model
- PDF research report
- later: RAG/LLM extension
